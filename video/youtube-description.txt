Learning to Fix a Wrong Answer: SCoRe and LLM Self-Correction

An individual research short story by Siddharth Rao.
A review of Training Language Models to Self-Correct via Reinforcement Learning (Kumar et al., 2024), with survey context, architecture, benchmark comparisons, ablations, metrics, and an LLMOps evaluation proposal.

Synthetic male narration. AI assisted the writing and visuals. All research results belong to the cited authors; this is not an independent training replication.
